How It Works

Step 1: Upload an image using the upload section above.

Step 2: The system processes the image using OpenCV and Pillow.

Step 3: It analyzes patterns, noise, and metadata.

Step 4: Based on analysis, it predicts whether the image is AI-generated or real.

Step 5: Displays the result with a confidence level.
